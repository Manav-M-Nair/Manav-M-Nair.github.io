Whenever I find a concept in Physics or Math particularly fascinating, I make notes of it in LaTeX. It’s a time-consuming habit, but it has proved to be a very effective way for me to learn. Unlike physical notebooks which have a tendency to get lost in space-time and are practically impossible to edit, LaTeX allows my notes to evolve alongside my understanding of the subject. I also feel genuine happiness about sharing these notes with others despite often being discouraged for my 'communist' behavior that is "claimed to be" increasing the class-median, but I can't help it: Physics and Mathematics are just too beautiful to be kept a secret.
